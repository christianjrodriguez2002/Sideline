# Sideline

Made by Roki Rodriguez.

A personal, all-sports Android app built on ESPN's public data. No account is required,
no API keys are required, no ads, no tracking is done. Everything stays on your phone.

## Sports

| | Leagues |
| --- | --- |
| **US Pro** | NFL · NBA · MLB · NHL · WNBA · UFL |
| **Soccer** | Premier League · MLS · Champions League · LaLiga · Bundesliga · Serie A · Ligue 1 |
| **International soccer** | FIFA World Cup · UEFA Euro · Copa América |

## What it does

- **Home**: pinned leagues, a live-and-today rail, and a card for every team and player you follow with their record,
  live scores, next game (TV, streaming, win chance), last result, injuries and headlines.
- **Scores**: a Today view with every game across your leagues, plus each league's day/week strip. Live games refresh
  on their own, and football schedules mark bye weeks.
- **Leagues**: every sport by category, each with scores, standings, playoff picture and bracket, rankings, stat
  leaders, teams, injuries, news and moves.
- **Team pages**: schedule and results with recent form, roster, season stats, standings, injuries, news. Add a
  team's whole season to your phone's calendar. Changed times update the next time you open the team.
- **Game pages**: score header in team colors, scoring by period, win probability with a chart of how it swung, where
  to watch, betting line, live situation, play by play, football drives, probable pitchers, leaders, team stats,
  lineups, box score, injuries, recent form and head-to-head. Finished games show ESPN's recap in the app. Share the
  score as an image or add the game to your calendar.
- **Live graphics**: pitch-by-pitch strike zone with the count, runners, matchup and fielders for baseball (between
  innings it shows who's taking the field and the next three batters due up), a drive
  field for football, shot charts for basketball and hockey, formations on a pitch for soccer, and who's serving in
  tennis.
- **Highlights (experimental)**: ESPN's latest clips, played in the app, for NFL, NBA, WNBA, NHL, college football and
  basketball, LaLiga and Bundesliga games. May be removed in a future update.
- **Photos (experimental)**: game and event photos from ESPN's recaps and team and league news, full screen with
  captions and credits. Mostly posted after the game. May be removed in a future update.
- **Player pages**: bio, injury status, season and career stats, next game, news. Follow a player to add them to Home,
  or compare two players side by side.
- **Golf scorecards**: tap a golfer on the leaderboard to see hole by hole scores when ESPN posts them.
- **Search**: teams, players and leagues from the search button in the top bar.
- **Home screen widgets**: your teams, a single team, a league's games today, and your team's standings.
- **Pull down to refresh** on any list, doing this will also make Sideline check for updates.
- **Game alerts**: optional notifications in Settings for games starting soon, lineups, your team scoring, close
  games, a live score while your team plays, and final scores.
- **Look**: dark or light theme, three text sizes, compact mode, and a two-column layout on tablets.
- **Screen readers**: TalkBack reads every button, section heading and live graphic, and reordered lists have Move up
  and Move down actions.
- **Offline**: the last scores you loaded still show without a connection.
- **Backup**: save your teams, players and settings to a file and restore them on a new phone.

## Your time and place

Every time follows your phone's time zone and updates as you travel. Game pages also show the clock at the venue when it differs.

Set your location in Settings (use the phone's location or type a city or ZIP) and the app works out which local
channels reach you:

- National TV shows the network and the app it usually streams on, with a "Find it on your TV" link for
  over-the-air stations.
- Regional sports networks show as your local channel when you're in that team's area, or as blacked out with the
  league's out-of-market package when you're not.

TV areas are approximate. Blackouts and your provider still apply.

## Photos and logos

Header photos are real photographs from Wikimedia Commons under free licenses, credited in Settings. League and team
logos and player photos come from ESPN.

## Notes

- ESPN's feeds are public but unofficial. If a feed changes, that screen may come up empty until the parser is
  updated. Every screen has a retry, and one broken item never takes down a list.
- Sideline collects zero personal data and stores all preferences locally on this device.
- For personal use. Team names and logos belong to their leagues and teams.
