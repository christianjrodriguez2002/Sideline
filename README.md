# Sideline

Made by Roki Rodriguez.

A personal, all-sports Android app with a dark, broadcast-style look. Built on ESPN's public data feeds: no account,
no API key, no ads, no tracking. Everything you set up stays on your phone.

| | Leagues |

## What it does

- **Home**: greeting, pinned leagues, a live-and-today rail, and a card for every team and player you follow.
- **Today**: every game across your leagues in one list, live first, with TV.
- **Scores**: each league's day or week, with bye weeks marked for football.
- **Leagues**: scores, standings, playoff picture and bracket, rankings, stat leaders, teams, injuries, news and moves.
- **Team pages**: schedule, roster, season stats, standings, injuries, news.
- **Game pages**: score header, win probability, where to watch, betting line, play by play, probable pitchers,
  leaders, team stats, lineups, box score, recent form, head to head. Share the score as an image or add the game
  to your calendar.
- **Player pages**: bio, stats, next game, news. Follow a player to put them on Home.
- **Search**: teams, players and leagues from one box.
- **Alerts**: starting soon, lineups, your team scores, close games, a live score notification, and finals.
- **Widgets**: your teams, one team, a league's day, and your team's standings.
- **Look**: dark or light, three text sizes, compact mode, and a two-column layout on tablets.
- **Offline**: the last scores show even without a connection.
- **Backup**: save your teams, players and settings to a file and restore them on a new phone.

## Your time and place

Every time follows your phone's time zone and updates as you travel. "Today" means your today, even though ESPN files
games under US Eastern dates. Game pages also show the clock at the venue when it differs.

Set your location in Settings (use the phone's location or type a city or ZIP) and the app works out which local
channels reach you:

- National TV shows the network and the app it usually streams on, with a "Find it on your TV" link for
  over-the-air stations.
- Regional sports networks show as your local channel when you're in that team's area, or as blacked out with the
  league's out-of-market package when you're not.

TV areas are approximate. Blackouts and your provider still apply.

## Photos and logos

Header photos are real photographs from Wikimedia Commons under free licenses, credited in Settings. League and team
logos and player photos come from ESPN.

## Install

Download the latest `Sideline-<version>.apk` from Releases and open it on your phone. Every build is signed with the
same key, so a new version installs over the previous one.

## Notes

- ESPN's feeds are public but unofficial. If a feed changes, that screen may come up empty until the parser is
  updated. Every screen has a retry, and one broken item never takes down a list.
- Sideline collects zero personal data and stores all preferences locally on this device.
- For personal use. Team names and logos belong to their leagues and teams.
